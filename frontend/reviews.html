FAQ

Questions customers may ask.

This page includes extra customer service content for a real store feel.

Is this a real payment system?

Checkout is connected to the store API and can redirect to Paystack when the payment secret is configured.

Can products be changed?

Yes. Product names, prices, descriptions, stock, and images are stored in the database for administration.

Does the cart work?

Yes. The cart works across pages, while orders are saved to the backend database.

Can it become a full website?

Yes. The backend already includes persistent products, customers, orders, inventory and payment verification foundations.
